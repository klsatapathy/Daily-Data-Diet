🥗 Day 6 of 90: I used to think Machine Learning was basically a fancy wizard behind a curtain, instantly knowing all the answers the second you flipped a switch. Turns out, it's a lot less like magic and a lot more like raising a very eager, slightly confused golden retriever.

When I first started trying to wrap my head around ML this week, I felt completely overwhelmed by the math and the jargon. I kept picturing a supercomputer plotting world domination. But then it clicked when I stopped thinking about code and started thinking about teaching.

Imagine you want to teach a kid how to spot a cat. You don't hand them a textbook on feline anatomy. Instead, you point at a furry neighborhood stray and say "cat," then point at a golden retriever and say "dog." You do this a few thousand times. Eventually, without you ever giving them a strict rulebook, their brain connects the dots. They notice pointy ears, whiskers, and tail-swishing. That’s machine learning in a nutshell.
