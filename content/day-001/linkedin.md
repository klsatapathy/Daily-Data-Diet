🥗 Day 1: The Mirror vs. The Crystal Ball 🔮

Stop confusing Data Science with Data Analytics!

Analytics looks at the past (what happened?); Science predicts the future (what will happen?).

If you're starting your data journey, you've probably seen these terms used interchangeably. But they aren't the same thing. Think of it like driving a car:

📊 Data Analytics is the Rearview Mirror. It looks at the past.
Question: "How many shoes did we sell last month?"
Goal: To understand trends and summarize what already happened.
Tools: Excel, SQL, Tableau.

🧪 Data Science is the GPS and Weather Forecast. It looks at the future.
Question: "Based on past sales, how many shoes will we sell next December?"
Goal: To build models that predict what will happen.
Tools: Python (our star ingredient!), Machine Learning, Statistics.

Why "The Data Diet" starts with Python? Python is the bridge. Whether you are analyzing the past or predicting the future, Python's libraries (like Pandas and Scikit-Learn) are the tools that make it possible.

Today's Bite-Sized Takeaway:
Analytics explains the "What" and "Why."
Science predicts the "When" and "How."
Both are vital, but Data Science is where we teach the computer to "think" ahead.
